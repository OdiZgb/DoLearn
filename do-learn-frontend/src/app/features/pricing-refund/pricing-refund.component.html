Pricing & Refund Policy

Pricing

At DoLearn, you only pay for what you need. Our courses are priced per session, giving you the flexibility to learn at your own pace without long-term commitments.

  • Each session price is clearly displayed before you book.
  • No hidden fees or recurring subscriptions.
  • Discounts and special offers may be available during promotions – keep an eye out!

Refund & Reschedule Policy

We want you to feel confident when you book. That's why we offer a straightforward policy if something unexpected happens:

  • If a teacher cannot deliver a scheduled session due to technical issues or unforeseen events, you can choose between:
    • 100% refund to your original payment method.
    • Free reschedule of the session at your convenience.
  • To request a refund or reschedule, please contact us within 24 hours of the affected session atDoLearnWebsite@gmail.com.
  • Refunds are typically processed within 5–7 business days.

We’re committed to making your learning experience smooth and worry-free. Thank you for choosing DoLearn!
